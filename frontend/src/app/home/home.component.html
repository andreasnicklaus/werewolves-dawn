Hope you survive the night

A village, a night, and a few wolves among you. Gather your table and survive until morning.

How to play
shield_moon

Better keep one eye open tonight.

For weeks, dead animals have been turning up in the woods around your village. Someone, or something, hunts every night. This morning the eldest swears she saw them: werewolves. Tonight everyone stays awake. Tomorrow you vote, and the lives of the whole village depend on it.

The village’s youngest asks the question no one wants to answer: if I am awake tonight, won’t you all think I might be a werewolf? Will you sleep, or risk your own life to find the evil?

How a round flows

01

Setup & deal

Choose the roles. Every player gets a secret role, and two extra cards go to the middle.

02

Night

Everyone closes their eyes. Roles wake in turn and play their action in secret. Each role gets 20 seconds per acting player, and advances early once everyone in that role group is done.

03

Dawn

The night ends. Roles may have changed during the night, and the village wakes up.

04

Day

The village debates and votes to remove one player. Five minutes is the suggested time (you manage it yourselves).

05

Reckoning

A werewolf voted out means the villagers win. A villager means the werewolves win. Points are awarded, then the next round begins.
